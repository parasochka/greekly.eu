Five ways to practise

One app, five modes, minutes a day.

Every session is short and focused. Every answer comes with an explanation, so a mistake becomes a mini-lesson instead of a red cross.

  • Quiz

    20 questions per session on grammar, vocabulary and word order, with a short explanation after every answer.

  • Dialogue

    An everyday conversation, from the taverna to the pharmacy, one line at a time.

  • Letter

    Write a short message in Greek and get it corrected.

  • Words

    Everyday vocabulary with repetition-first review and audio pronunciation.

  • Sentence constructor

    Build Greek sentences from tiles and learn the word order.

FAQ

Good to know

Which Greek does Greekly teach?

Modern Greek, the language spoken in Greece and Cyprus today. It is not a course in Ancient, Koine or Biblical Greek.

What level is it for?

Beginners. Topics follow a structured ladder from the alphabet and first phrases to everyday conversation. Greekly is a practice tool, not a teacher and not a certification.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. Sign in with Apple is optional and only exists so your progress can follow you to another iPhone. No email is required.

What is included in the free plan?

5 exercises a day for your first 6 days, in all five modes. Greekly Monthly then unlocks unlimited practice. Price, length and renewal terms are shown on the subscription screen before you buy.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app or your Greekly account does not cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Can the AI make mistakes?

Most exercises are generated by artificial intelligence. Despite review, occasional inaccuracies are possible. If you spot one, use “Report a problem” inside the app: it helps Greekly improve quickly.
