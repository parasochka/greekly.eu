Basic Greek phrases: 70 you will use every day

Greetings, polite words, ordering food, shopping, directions and emergencies, in Greek script with a simple pronunciation guide. Stressed syllables are marked with an accent.

Updated September 30, 2026 · WCBO LLC

The ten to learn first

Γεια σας (ya sas, hello) · Καλημέρα (kaliméra, good morning) · Ευχαριστώ (efkharistó, thank you) · Παρακαλώ (parakaló, please / you're welcome) · Ναι (ne, yes) · Όχι (óhi, no) · Συγγνώμη (sighnómi, sorry) · Δεν καταλαβαίνω (dhen katalavéno, I don't understand) · Πόσο κάνει; (póso káni?, how much is it?) · Τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ (ton loghariazmó, parakaló, the bill, please)

How to read the pronunciation

The pronunciation column uses simple Latin letters. The accent shows the stressed syllable, and stress matters in Greek. A few letters need a note:

  • dh = the “th” in this (Greek δ); th = the “th” in think (θ).
  • gh = a soft, breathy “g” (γ before a, o, u); before e and i it sounds like y.
  • kh = the “ch” in Scottish loch (χ).
  • r is a short tap, as in Spanish or Italian.

Greek writes its question mark like an English semicolon: Τι κάνεις; is “How are you?”.

From phrases to real Greek

Phrases get you through the first week. To go further you need the patterns behind them: why it is έναν καφέ but ένα νερό, or τι κάνεις with a friend but τι κάνετε with a pharmacist. That is grammar, and it is the step that turns memorised lines into sentences of your own. Our guide How to learn Greek shows the order to learn it in, and the Greekly app gives you dialogues built around the places you actually go.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say hello in Greek?

Γεια σου (ya su) to one person you know, and Γεια σας (ya sas) to someone you address politely or to a group. Both also mean goodbye. Καλημέρα (kaliméra) means good morning and Καλησπέρα (kalispéra) good evening.

How do you say thank you in Greek?

Ευχαριστώ (efkharistó). Thank you very much is Ευχαριστώ πολύ (efkharistó polí). The usual reply is Παρακαλώ (parakaló), which also means please.

How do you say yes and no in Greek?

Yes is Ναι (ne) and no is Όχι (óhi). Ναι sounds like the English “neigh”, which confuses many visitors at first.

Why do Greeks use a semicolon as a question mark?

In Greek the question mark is written like the English semicolon (;). Τι κάνεις; means “How are you?”. A raised dot (·) plays the role of the English semicolon.

What is the most useful Greek phrase for beginners?

Συγγνώμη, δεν καταλαβαίνω (sighnómi, dhen katalavéno), “Sorry, I don't understand”, followed by Μπορείτε να μιλάτε πιο αργά; (boríte na miláte pio arghá?), “Could you speak more slowly?”. Together they keep a conversation going instead of ending it.

What is the emergency number in Greece and Cyprus?

112, the European emergency number, works in both Greece and Cyprus.
