What Greekly is

A tool for practising Modern Greek: quizzes, dialogues, writing practice, vocabulary, and sentence building. Most exercises are generated by an AI model. It is a practice tool, not a teacher and not a certification. Generated exercises and explanations can be wrong, and Greekly does not promise that any of it is accurate, complete, or enough to pass an examination. If something looks wrong, the “Report a problem” screen is the fastest way to get it fixed.

Greekly accounts are separate per product. An account created in the iOS app is not the same account as one created in a Telegram bot, and progress and subscriptions do not move between them. This is deliberate: they teach different material and each is bought separately.

Free plan and free trial

Two different things, and they are named differently on purpose.

  • The free plan is what you get before you subscribe: a limited number of exercises per day, for a limited number of days. The exact numbers are shown in the app and may change.
  • A free trial, where offered, is a free introductory period of the paid subscription itself, provided through the App Store. Its length and what happens when it ends are shown on the subscription screen before you confirm.

Subscriptions

The subscription unlocks unlimited practice. Its price, length and renewal terms are shown before you buy, on the screen where you buy it.

Bought in the iOS app

Payment is taken by Apple and charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the current period ends; your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. That is the only place it can be cancelled: deleting the app, or deleting your Greekly account, does not cancel it, and neither can be done on your behalf.

If a free trial is offered and you buy a subscription during it, any unused part of the trial is forfeited, which is Apple's rule for all apps.
